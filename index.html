BLUFF MARKET
You and 3 rivals fight over fighters at a live timed auction — the clock resets with every raise, so hold your nerve. Your gold is secret, so bluff: scare rivals off early or snipe at the buzzer. Matching unit types unlock synergy bonuses. Then your squads clash in an animated battle. Most points after 3 rounds takes the crown.
Difficulty:
Tip: rivals can see what you won and what you paid — but never how much gold you have left. Use that.
